# Complete API comparison

Five baseline/candidate runs per row. Timings are nanoseconds per call;
space rows are bytes. Lower is better. IQR is the 75th minus 25th percentile.
The paired percentage is the median of the five per-input candidate/baseline
changes, rather than a percentage formed from unrelated aggregate timings.
These pairs match input seeds, not alternating wall-clock runs: the final
candidate batch reuses the earlier five baseline runs. See the
[measurement method and independent guards](README.md#complete-comparisons).
All rows are retained, including regressions and unresolved variation.
