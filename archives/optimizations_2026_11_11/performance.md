# Complete API, memory and storage comparison

Five full runs of each build, interleaved original → baseline → candidate, then
reverse order. Original is `e138158`; baseline is `1c0a007`; candidate is this
round’s final build. All three builds use identical compiler flags, benchmarks,
fixed native Session entropy and five matched workload seeds (`editing-pair-0`
through `editing-pair-4`). The default workloads, call limits and checkpoints are
unchanged. Lifecycle, throughput and tuple are included: **424 timing rows and
96 memory/storage rows**. Normal README reports are separate three-run defaults.

Timing values are ns/call, median (IQR). IQR is the interpolated 75th percentile
minus the 25th percentile. Percentages compare medians; negative is better.
Paired percentages are the median (IQR) of five per-input candidate/baseline
latency changes. Medians and paired medians can differ substantially. Neither
a 5% cutoff nor overlapping IQRs prove equivalence. Input generation, transport
encoding and explicit JS process garbage collection are outside API timing.

The requested all-row criterion is **not established** by these measurements.
The broad suite contains apparent losses of previous gains, including 22 rows
where the old median was over 5% faster than original and the candidate median
is over 5% slower than original. Follow-up public ESM guards investigate reads,
creation, replication and tuples; they do not replace or erase these rows.
See [measurements](measurements.md) for both repetitions, A/A controls and limits.

The frozen timing build predates removal of an unused native range overload
and relocation/renaming of the identical cold error helper. Wasm and generated
glue remain byte-identical after cleanup. After removing that helper definition
and normalizing its name, both final ESM/CommonJS bundles exactly match the
measured bundles. The final source is tested again and its normal three-run
reports and artifact sizes are measured separately. Both artifact hashes are
recorded in the [round report](README.md).
