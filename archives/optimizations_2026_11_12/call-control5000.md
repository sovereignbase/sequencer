# call-control5000: production throughput control

Five matched rounds of all 39 production throughput cases at 5,000 starting Strips. Only the checkpoint selection differs from the complete-suite run. Warmup, work, seeds and timing limits are unchanged. Build order rotates and reverses; all samples, including slower results, are retained.

Times are median ns/call. Each IQR is the third minus first quartile. Paired changes compare matching seeds; negative means faster. Length is retained as a control and excluded from the timing acceptance criterion.
