# frontier-control: production throughput control

5 matched rounds of 11 production throughput cases at 1000, 10000, 25000 starting Strips. Case/checkpoint selection is recorded explicitly; preparation, warmup, work, checks and timing limits are unchanged. Build order rotates and reverses; all samples, including slower results, are retained.

Times are median ns/call. Each IQR is the third minus first quartile. Paired changes compare matching seeds; negative means faster. Length is retained as a control and excluded from the timing acceptance criterion.
