# kind-warm: production throughput control

Five matched rounds, baseline, identical baseline control and candidate. The 27
throughput rows above +10% in the complete guard are repeated with 10,000 warmup
calls instead of 64, identically for every build. Fixtures, seeds, assertions,
10,000-call measurement cap and 100 ms limit remain unchanged. This is a warmed
diagnostic, not a replacement for the original complete guard. Build order rotates
and reverses; every sample remains visible. Candidate median changes are all
below +10%; the largest is +8.80%. Paired changes and IQR remain separate.

Times are median ns/call. Each IQR is the third minus first quartile. Paired changes compare matching seeds; negative means faster. No length case is selected in this subset.
