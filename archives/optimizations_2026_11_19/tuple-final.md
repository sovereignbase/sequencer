# tuple-final: production throughput control

Five matched rounds of the complete unchanged tuple benchmark: ten methods at
2, 4, 8 and 100 indices, in ordinary and static-tuple modes. Each method warms
for at least 25 ms and measures at least 50 ms of API work. Baseline, identical
baseline control and candidate use the same fixtures and timing settings. Build
order rotates and reverses; all 80 rows and every sample remain visible.

Times are median ns/call. Each IQR is the third minus first quartile. Paired changes compare matching seeds; negative means faster. Length is retained as a control and excluded from the timing acceptance criterion.
